# Muse demo video script

Target length: 2 minutes.

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem and promise

Open on the Muse landing page.

> Beauty inspiration is everywhere, but it rarely explains whether a look will translate to your own features. Muse uses YouCam to turn one selfie into relevant celebrity references, virtual experiments, and a practical plan to recreate the result.

## 0:15–0:35 — Guided analysis

Show account entry, the selfie upload/capture choices, and Camera Kit guidance. Briefly show the animated analysis state and facial-analysis panel.

> Muse uses YouCam Fitzpatrick Skin Type, Facial Color Tones, and Face Attributes & Ratio Analysis. Detailed eyes, brows, nose, lips, cheekbones, face shape, and coloring drive the match—not a beauty score.

## 0:35–0:55 — Muse matrix

Open the five-muse result. Pan or zoom the overlapping collage and select one muse from the bottom dock.

> The profile is compared with a curated catalog of 103 muses, 1,856 unique images, and 412 hair and makeup looks. The five closest matches become a visual reference board.

## 0:55–1:25 — Virtual try-on

Move to Makeup, choose a reference, confirm the compatible base selfie, and show a completed look. Repeat quickly with Hair or show a saved combined branch.

> Any inspiration photo resolves to its approved hidden transfer template. YouCam renders the look on the selected selfie, and Muse saves every hair-and-makeup branch with its provenance. Failed source templates can be rebuilt once with YouCam Image Generator and cached for future users.

## 1:25–1:50 — Achieve this look

Open Achieve mode. Show the saved Skin Analysis, horizontal technique tutorial, essential-product checklist, owned-product assessment, and four varied Shopify recommendations.

> Muse keeps Skin Analysis tied to the untouched assessment selfie. It then turns a look into reusable technique steps and checks the products a member already owns before searching live Shopify merchants.

## 1:50–2:00 — Impact

Return to the generated portrait and sample-board entry point.

> Muse helps people borrow useful styling ideas while appreciating their own features—and gives beauty retailers a more relevant path from inspiration to experimentation to purchase.

## Capture checklist

- Record at 1080p with the browser at 100% zoom.
- Do not show API keys, database URLs, usernames, or passwords.
- Use a designated demo account and consented/generated selfie.
- Show one successful YouCam analysis result and at least one successful try-on.
- End on the live URL and repository name.
